# Text changes: less on-screen text, nothing useful lost

Every shortened, moved or corrected piece of on-screen text, as **before / after / where the information now lives**. "ⓘ" is the info hint (`InfoTip` in `src/components/ui.jsx`). It opens on hover, on keyboard focus, or with a click or tap (which keeps it open until clicked again, a tap elsewhere, or Escape). It stays within the screen or dialog, works in read-only forms, and screen readers announce its text.

**Rules applied:** disclaimers are kept in full (one copy each); facts are never dropped, only shortened or moved into an ⓘ; text that repeated something visible nearby (a heading, a tile, the buttons beside it) is removed and the table says where it is still shown.
